Performance

4K media, decoded in hardware.

Raspberry Pi 5. The quad‑core Arm Cortex‑A76 processor at 2.4 GHz and VideoCore VII GPU decode 4K video in hardware — H.265 (HEVC), VP9, H.264 and AV1 — so playback is smooth, silent and effortless, and the whole box sips a few watts of power.

Two 4K displays. Dual micro‑HDMI ports drive two screens at up to 4K p60 each — perfect for a living‑room TV and a second screen or projector.

Sound your way. Bit‑perfect audio passthrough over HDMI to your soundbar or AV receiver — including Dolby Digital, DTS and lossless TrueHD when your files carry them — or wireless audio over Bluetooth 5.

Play, pause, browse, type. Free Kodi remote apps give you a touchpad for navigation, a full on‑screen keyboard for search, and one‑tap access to subtitles, settings and your media library — all over your own Wi‑Fi network.

Works with any TV. StreamBox connects to any HDMI TV. Turn your TV on and off with CEC, and control volume from the app — or teach the box’s IR receiver to respond to the remote you already own.

Hardware

Powerful inside.
Quiet by design.

Under the hood is a Raspberry Pi 5 with 4GB of RAM, inside a custom‑built case with two whisper‑quiet fans — running LibreELEC, a purpose‑built operating system tuned for media playback.

Raspberry Pi 5
The heart of the box. Everything a media centre needs — a quad‑core Arm Cortex‑A76 processor at 2.4 GHz, 4GB of RAM, dual 4K HDMI output, Gigabit Ethernet, Wi‑Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5. Plays 4K video from USB drives, a NAS or your home network without breaking a sweat.

Fan‑cooled case
Cool and quiet. Two quiet fans keep the Pi at a healthy temperature through the longest film marathons — no thermal throttling, no distracting noise.

Open source
Your media stays yours. LibreELEC is built on open‑source software — no accounts, no ads, no data collection. Your library lives on your own drives, not in the cloud.
